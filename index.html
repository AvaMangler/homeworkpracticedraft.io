A Career in Radiologic Technology

Why Radiologic Technology

  • Contribute to patient diagnosis
  • Work in a hospital
  • Work with different modalities
  • Inspired by radiologic technologists
  • Interested in medical technology

Career Technology Goals

  • X-ray equipment and control panel
  • PACS software
  • RIS software
  • EMR software

Career Goals

Short Term Career Goals

  1. >2.5 GPA in Rad Tech
  2. C+ or higher in all RAD courses
  3. 5 clinical rotations
  4. CPR
  5. Pass ARRT

Long Term Career Goals

  1. Certified radiologic technologist
  2. Cross-train CT/MRI
  3. Clinical instructor
  4. Lead tech / department administrator
