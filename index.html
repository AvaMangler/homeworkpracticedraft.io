A Career in Radiologic Technology

Why I want to be rad tech

  • Contribute to patient diagnosis
  • Work in a hospital
  • Work with different modalities
  • Inspired by radiologic technologists
  • Interested in medical technology

Career tech goals

Learn how to use:

  • X-ray equipment and control panel
  • PACS software
  • RIS software
  • EMR software

Short term career goals

  1. Graduate with >2.5 GPA in Rad Tech program
  2. Maintain C+ or higher in all RAD courses
  3. Complete 5 clinical rotations
  4. Earn CPR certification
  5. Pass the ARRT certification exam

Long term career goals

  • Become a certified radiologic technologist
  • Work as a radiographer
  • Cross train in CT/MRI
  • Become a lead tech
  • Take on an instructor role
