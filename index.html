A Career in Radiologic Technology

Introduction

Why I Want to Be a Radiologic Technologist

I want to contribute to patient diagnosis, work in a hospital, work with different modalities, and use medical technology. I was also inspired by radiologic technologists.

Technical Skills and Software

I want to learn how to use the following equipment and software:

  • X-ray equipment and control panel
  • PACS software
  • RIS software
  • EMR software

Short-Term Career Goals

  1. Graduate from the Radiologic Technology program with a GPA above 2.5.
  2. Maintain a C+ or higher in all RAD courses.
  3. Complete five clinical rotations.
  4. Earn CPR certification.
  5. Pass the ARRT certification exam.

Long-Term Career Goals

  • Become a certified radiologic technologist.
  • Work as a radiographer.
  • Cross-train in CT and MRI.
  • Become a lead technologist.
  • Take on an instructor role.

Skills I Want to Develop

  • Radiographic positioning
  • Patient care
  • Equipment operation
  • Radiation safety
  • Adaptability
  • Physical fitness
